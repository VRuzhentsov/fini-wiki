# awesome-tauri

Officially maintained curated list of [[tauri]]-related projects:
applications, plugins, guides, and tutorials. Follows the standard
"awesome-*" GitHub list convention.

**URL:** https://github.com/tauri-apps/awesome-tauri

Per [[sources/2024-10-14-tauri-2-released]], the list is intended as
a discovery surface for new Tauri users ("if you're looking for
inspiration") and as a place to advertise your own project. The
article specifically links the `#applications` section.

## Why note it

For a framework where the "can I actually build X with this?"
question is central, awesome-tauri is the fastest answer — shipping
apps are the strongest existence proof of capability, and a curated
list is denser signal than GitHub trending.

## Open questions

> [!question] How current is the list kept? Awesome-lists vary
> wildly in maintenance; the quality of the signal depends on it.

> [!question] Are Tauri 2.0-specific apps (especially mobile) called
> out separately, or mixed with 1.x apps?
